# Lobbywatch data attribution

The records in this directory are adapted from the weekly public data export by [Lobbywatch Switzerland](https://lobbywatch.ch/datenexport/).

Lobbywatch licenses the export under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Verflecht selects relationship fields, maps them to its controlled ontology, joins parliamentarians to Swiss Parliament identifiers, and labels every resulting claim `PENDING_REVIEW`.

This notice applies to the Lobbywatch-derived data in this directory, not automatically to unrelated project code or independently sourced data.
